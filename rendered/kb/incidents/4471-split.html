#4471 was merged by subject line (decision 0001). Every report says “wrong date”, so every report landed in one ticket. Its page says one issue, one fix queued. The reports say three.

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October: the parser fix shipped (fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales), and the booking-v3 form was retired, so its hint is won’t fix.

#4471 is closed, but one problem in it is still open: bookings that shift one day across time zones. Neither the parser fix nor the form retirement touches it. Keep its ticket open.

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. 2 March goes out as 3 February. Fixed 5 October.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the field day first. People type what the hint says and get a swapped date. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Won’t fix: the form is retired.
  • shift: not a swap. The date moves back one day, and only for some readers: Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday. That is a time-zone bug. The desk noted these as one-day moves and counts them under parser. Still open.

How each report was placed

Counted with the desk’s own view, as exported (causes.ts, the same logic as CauseTable):

  1. One id, one report: the export repeats some rows across pages.
  2. The tenant decides: a form report from a booking-v3 tenant is hint; any other report from a day-first tenant is parser.
  3. A report the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) stays counted under its tenant’s cause, and also makes the shift row.

The intake tag is not used. Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser, and the browser is not the tenant.

Lessons

  • A shared subject is not a shared cause. Count by cause before you call a ticket known.
  • Look past the biggest row. The largest group sets the ticket’s title; the smaller ones disappear under it.
